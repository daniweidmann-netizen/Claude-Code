# PARTNERSHIP OPTIONS

Date: 25 November 2026 · internal preparation · no prices, no benchmarks, no offer exists

| | Publishing partnership | Exclusive licence | Non-exclusive licence | Full IP acquisition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What happens | provider distributes; developer remains the studio | one provider gets sole rights for a period and scope | several providers may license the game | all rights and files pass to the buyer |
| Typical money flow (structure only) | revenue share, possibly an advance | upfront fee and ongoing share | fee and/or share per licensee | one price, possibly in instalments or with an earn-out |
| Developer's ongoing work | high: integration, certification support, maintenance | medium | medium to high (several integrations) | low after hand-over |
| Opportunity | recurring income, a studio track record, follow-up titles | income plus reversion rights | several channels | immediate, closed result |
| Risk | income uncertain; heavy workload for one person | tied up during exclusivity | less attractive to providers; more support load | no share in later success; strictest demands on rights |
| Rights position today (see `PROJECT_AUDIT/IP/`) | RED with today's assets; workable if the provider replaces art and audio and the code question is cleared | RED – exclusivity cannot be warranted for art, audio; code unclear | RED with today's assets; exclusivity not needed, which helps | RED – ownership free of encumbrances cannot be warranted |
| What would make it viable | written provider confirmations or replaced assets; legal clarity on the code | additionally a trade mark and contract wording "as far as rights exist" | as publishing | additionally full chain of title, trade mark, replaced assets |

## Reading

- The option with the fewest legal obstacles is the one in which the partner brings its own art
  and audio and takes concept, mathematics and engine.
- The more exclusive and final the deal, the more the unresolved rights weigh.
- A trade mark would be the one clearly transferable exclusive right the project could obtain
  with modest effort.

## Decisions the owner must make

Preferred model and fallback · willingness to do integration work · whether to invest in
commissioned art and audio before or after first feedback · legal form.
