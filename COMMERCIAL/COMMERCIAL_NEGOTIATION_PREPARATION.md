# COMMERCIAL NEGOTIATION PREPARATION

Date: 25 November 2026 · **internal – do not send** · contains no price expectations by design

> No industry benchmarks are given here: the project holds no reliable data on advances, revenue
> shares or purchase prices, and none are invented. The owner's own minimum acceptable terms must
> be kept in a separate private note and never placed in a document that leaves the house.

## 1. What is being offered

Concept and name · math engine with configuration 1.0.0, simulator, reference implementation and
tests · demo client · documentation. Art and audio as presentation assets with unresolved rights.

## 2. Strengths to lead with [FACT]

- Complete, tested game loop with a distinct mechanic.
- Mathematics measured on the shipped configuration with reproducible evidence and a second implementation.
- Engine separated from presentation; deterministic; replayable.
- Transparent documentation, including the weak points.

## 3. Weak points a provider will find – and the honest answer

| Likely question | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| Who validated the math? | Nobody independent. Internal simulation; documentation is ready for a laboratory. |
| Which RTP variants? | One. More can be produced with the existing tooling. |
| Who owns the art, audio and code? | Created with AI services under my direction. Origin is fully documented; commercial and exclusive rights are under legal review. Art and audio can be replaced. |
| Does it run on our platform? | The engine is portable; there is no server integration yet. |
| Which devices? | Automated tests in Chromium; my own iPhone 15 Pro. A device matrix is open. |
| Track record? | None. First title, single developer. |
| Why is the buy feature RTP higher? | A deliberate round price; it is disclosed and can be changed or disabled. |
| Was AI used? | Yes, for code, art and audio. This is stated in the documentation. |

## 4. Questions to ask the provider

1. Is your own client framework mandatory? Which RGS interface?
2. Which RTP variants and markets do you expect?
3. Who carries certification and its cost?
4. What is your position on AI-generated assets; would you replace art and audio?
5. What do you expect from a studio after launch (support, updates)?
6. Which models do you offer to a first-time studio?

## 5. Negotiation points by model (structure only)

| Model | Points |
|---|---|
| Publishing | advance; basis of the revenue share; term; territories; who pays for certification and art rework; follow-up titles |
| Exclusive licence | scope and duration; minimum guarantee; reversion if not released; change rights |
| Non-exclusive licence | per-licensee terms; support load; brand control |
| IP acquisition | scope of transfer; payment plan; warranties limited to what the audit supports; credit; hand-over support |

## 6. Red lines for warranties

Never warrant: copyright ownership of AI-generated art, audio or code; exclusivity in art and audio;
that the name is free as a trade mark; certification; device compatibility beyond what was tested.

## 7. Before any term sheet

Lawyer engaged · legal form decided · evidence folder complete · private note with own minimum
terms prepared (kept out of all shared material).
