# 11 – Gameplay Trailer Concept (45 seconds)

Format 16:9 master with a 9:16 cut; all footage captured from the real game (no mock-ups), music from the game's free-spin loop, no voice-over. On-screen text in the game's display font.

| Time | Picture | Text on screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Slow push-in on the jungle temple background, dust in the light; logo fades in | TEMPLE OF THE LOST SUN | Base-game music, low |
| 4–7 s | Tagline under the logo | EVERY RELIC REVEALS A SECRET. | Soft bell |
| 7–13 s | Portrait gameplay: a cluster lights up, dissolves, symbols fall; the track fills its first stone | EVERY WIN IS A STEP | Win chime, cascade |
| 13–20 s | Fast cuts of the five medallions unlocking, each with its own gesture | DISCOVERY · TREASURE · ANCIENT POWER · RELIC · TEMPLE | One accent per station, rising |
| 20–27 s | The gate opens into the temple interior; counter and growing sun crown on the multiplier | FREE SPINS · THE SUN BONUS STAYS | Fanfare, music switches to the free-spin loop |
| 27–36 s | Temple Bonus: three doors, a finger taps one, the door opens in light, treasure rises, amount counts | THREE CHAMBERS · YOUR CHOICE DECIDES | Door, swell |
| 36–41 s | Big-win presentation with sunburst and coins | WIN UP TO 10,000× | Big-win sting |
| 41–45 s | Logo on dark stone | Logo, line "Demo footage. Simulated RTP 96.00 % target, not certified." in small type | Music resolves |

Production notes

- Capture with fixed seeds so every shot is repeatable (document 10); record at 60 fps, device pixel ratio 2.
- Use only shots where no interface text is cut off; check each frame.
- No claims about odds beyond the maximum win, and keep the "not certified" line legible.
- Needs: final decision on audio after it has been reviewed by ear; a vector logo for the end card.
