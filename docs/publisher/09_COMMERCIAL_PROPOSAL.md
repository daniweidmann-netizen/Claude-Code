# 09 – Commercial Proposal (draft for discussion)

> Prepared by the developer as a starting point. No figures are proposed here: appropriate advances and revenue shares depend on the partner's programme and market data the developer does not have. Nothing in this document is an offer capable of acceptance.

## What is offered

- The game design and name TEMPLE OF THE LOST SUN.
- The math engine with configuration 1.0.0, simulator, Python reference implementation and test suite.
- The demo client (Phaser 3) with all current art and audio.
- Documentation: this package and the internal design and math documents.

## Models we are open to

| Model | What it means | Our preference |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Studio partnership** – advance plus ongoing revenue share | The partner distributes the game on its platform; we remain the studio of record, deliver the game to the partner's technical requirements and receive an advance against a share of net gaming revenue. | **Preferred** |
| B. Licence | Exclusive or time-limited licence of game and math; the partner builds or adapts the client. Fee plus revenue share. | Open |
| C. Full IP sale | One-time transfer of all rights in design, math, code and brand. | Possible |

## What a partner should know before valuing it

1. **Not certified.** Certification and jurisdiction-specific compliance would be done on the partner's platform with its RNG.
2. **One RTP configuration.** Additional RTP variants need new parameter sets and new simulation runs (the tooling exists).
3. **Rights are not cleared.** Art and audio are AI-generated without human rework; the providers' terms have not been legally reviewed; no trademark search in registers. Any of the three models needs this resolved or priced in. A partner may prefer to replace art and audio with its own.
4. **Client technology.** The demo runs on Phaser 3. If the partner's platform requires its own framework, the engine and the event log are designed to be reused while the client is rebuilt.
5. **Team.** A single developer; no shipped titles.

## What we would need from a partner

- Technical requirements of the programme (framework, RGS interface, certification path, RTP variants expected).
- Commercial terms of the programme as they apply to a first title.
- Feedback on whether the theme and mechanic fit the current portfolio.

## Open questions on our side

- Legal form under which the owner would contract.
- Whether to commission human artwork and music before or after a partner's feedback.
