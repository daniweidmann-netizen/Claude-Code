# 04 – Feature Overview

## Expedition Track (signature feature)

**What the player sees.** Five medallions above the grid: Discovery (torch), Treasure (chest), Ancient Power (jade eye), Relic (sun disc), Temple (gate). Stepping stones between them fill as the round progresses; a line of text always names the next station and the steps still needed.

**How it works.** Every winning cascade is one step; every Temple Key on the grid is one more. Stations are reached after 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 9 steps and apply their effect at once. The track starts again on every paid spin.

**Why it is more than a meter.** Each station changes the game state in a different way – two Wilds, a doubled multiplier, a Wild that stays, a multiplier boost that turns into a Wild, the bonus – so a long cascade tells a small story with rising stakes. The player can always see how far the current spin has come and what the next step would unlock.

**Measured** (2 billion rounds): highest station per base spin – none 67.2 %, Discovery 7.2 %, Treasure 19.2 %, Ancient Power 4.6 %, Relic 1.5 %, Temple 0.31 %.

## Temple Bonus

Three chambers – Sun, Jade, Moon – each with its own door. The three prizes are drawn and placed before the player chooses; the choice decides the win; the other chambers are opened afterwards and shown as they were. A Relic Bonus (25×) grants a second choice.

**Why it is interesting.** It is a real pick, not a staged one, and the game says so on screen. Every chamber has the same expectation, which is provable from the configuration (document 02). That is a trust argument that can be stated plainly in the rules.

**Measured:** triggered 1 in 326 base rounds; average prize 50.8× bet (exact expectation 50.97×); contributes 15.6 percentage points of RTP.

## Free Spins with Base Camp

3+ Compass award 8–12 free spins. Two things carry the feature:

- **Base Camp.** Each free spin starts on a station, with its effects already applied. Temple Keys push the camp forward, up to Ancient Power – so later spins start with Wilds, ×2 and an Explorer.
- **Sun bonus.** The multiplier's sun part is kept for the whole feature and only grows.

Reaching the Temple during free spins earns a seal; with at least one seal the feature ends in a **Temple Finale** with larger prizes (grand treasure up to 10,000× with three seals).

**Measured:** 1 in 222 rounds; average feature win 67.9× bet; final Base Camp on station 3 in 47.8 % of features; at least one seal in 13.0 %.

## Buy Expedition

Starts the same feature a 3-Compass trigger starts, for 58× bet. Simulated RTP 96.81 %, shown separately in the rules. Can be disabled by configuration for markets that do not allow feature buys.

## Win cap and presentation

Maximum win 10,000× bet; reached about once in 3 million rounds in simulation. Presentation tiers (display only): from 5×, 20× and 100× bet.

## Player-facing transparency

Rules and paytable generated from the live configuration; round history with seed and exact replay; simulated RTP labelled as not certified; speed-up and autoplay documented as having no effect on results.
