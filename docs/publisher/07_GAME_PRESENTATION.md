# 07 – Game Presentation

A walk-through for a 15-minute meeting. Each block is one talking point with what to show in the demo.

## 1. The premise (1 minute)

An expedition into a lost sun temple. The player is not spinning reels; the player is moving along a path. **Show:** start screen, then the idle screen with the five medallions above the grid.

## 2. The core loop (3 minutes)

6×5, clusters of five or more, cascades. Every winning cascade is a step on the Expedition Track. **Show:** play base spins until a spin reaches Treasure. Point at the stepping stones, the line "Next: Ancient Power · 2 steps", the beam from the station into the grid.

What to say: every station does something different, so a long cascade has a shape – Wilds, then a doubled multiplier, then a Wild that stays, then a multiplier that only goes up, then the temple.

## 3. Free spins (4 minutes)

**Show:** Buy Expedition. The gate opens, the scenery changes to the temple interior, the counter takes the headline.

What to say: two ideas. Base Camp – every free spin starts on a station, and keys push the camp forward. Sun bonus – the multiplier keeps what it collects for the whole feature. Together they make the last spins of a feature the most valuable ones.

## 4. Temple Bonus (3 minutes)

**Show:** a bonus round (in the demo, a seed that leads to one; see document 10). Three doors, the rule text on screen, the door opening, the other doors opening afterwards.

What to say: it is a real choice. The prizes are placed before the pick, every chamber has the same expectation, and the game shows afterwards what the other chambers held. That can be written into the rules and defended.

## 5. Numbers (2 minutes)

Simulated RTP 95.995 % over two billion rounds; hit frequency 32.8 %; free spins about 1 in 222; Temple Bonus about 1 in 326; maximum win 10,000×, reached about once in three million rounds. **Say clearly:** simulated, not certified; a second independent implementation agrees round for round.

## 6. What we are asking for (2 minutes)

A technical and commercial conversation: does the game fit the programme, and what would an integration require – port to the partner's framework, or integration of the existing engine behind a remote game server? See document 09.

## What not to claim

- Not certified, not approved in any jurisdiction.
- Not tested on real devices yet (as of this document).
- Art and audio are AI-generated and have not been cleared legally.
