## Still required (blocking for Chapter 01 / 04)

2. Vector logo (SVG / AI) — the wordmark with the two-leaf "i".
3. At least 6 real photographs of the physical pouch (front, back, ¾ left, ¾ right, top, side) on neutral background, plus pouch dimensions in mm.
4. Macro photos of 3–5 real gummies (top, side, cut) + dimensions.
5. Full back-of-pack text: ingredient list, nutrition / per-portion table, recommended intake, warnings, the `*` footnote text, producer address.

Put them in `references/packaging/`, `references/logo/`, `references/photos/`, `references/label-text/`.
