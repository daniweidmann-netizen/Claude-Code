# GAME FEATURE OVERVIEW

**TEMPLE OF THE LOST SUN** · for provider product teams · date: 25 November 2026
Frequencies are internal simulation results (2 billion rounds), not certified. Detailed values
(paytable, weights, prize tables) are available under a non-disclosure agreement.

## Core game
6 columns × 5 rows. Clusters of five or more equal symbols connected horizontally or vertically
win. Winning symbols are removed and new symbols fall in; cascades continue while clusters form.
Five pay symbols: Sapphire Gem, Golden Scarab, Golden Chalice, Ruby Crown, Jade Mask.

## Special symbols
- **Wild** – substitutes for every pay symbol. It never falls onto the grid; it is created by the
  expedition.
- **Explorer** – a Wild that stays in place after wins until the spin ends.
- **Compass** – three or more at the end of a spin trigger Free Spins.
- **Temple Key** – each key in a winning cascade adds a step on the Expedition Track.

## Expedition System (signature feature)
Every winning cascade is one step. Stations are reached after 1, 2, 4, 6 and 9 steps:

| Station | Effect | Reached as highest station |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | two pay symbols turn into Wilds | 7.2 % of base spins |
| Treasure | base multiplier ×2 for the rest of the spin | 19.2 % |
| Ancient Power | one symbol becomes the Explorer | 4.6 % |
| Relic | the Sun Relic adds its value to the multiplier and turns into a Wild | 1.5 % |
| Temple | Temple Bonus | 0.31 % (about 1 in 326) |

The Sun Relic is the game's central artefact, not a reel symbol.

## Free Spins
8, 10 or 12 spins for 3, 4 or 5+ Compass; retrigger +4, up to 30. Each free spin starts on a Base
Camp station with the lower stations already active; Temple Keys move the camp up. The sun bonus
is kept across the feature. Reaching the Temple collects seals for the Temple Finale.
About 1 in 222 rounds.

## Temple Bonus and Temple Finale
Three chambers, one choice; prizes are set before the choice and the others are revealed
afterwards. A Relic Bonus grants another chamber. The Finale after Free Spins offers larger
treasures, up to the 10,000× maximum win with three seals.

## Buy Expedition (optional)
Buys the Free-Spin feature for 58× bet. Shown with its own RTP in the rules; can be disabled per
market.

## Player comfort
Normal, Fast and Skip speed · autoplay · history with replay of every round · rules and paytable
in the game · reduced-motion support · portrait and landscape.
