### Slide 1 – Title
**TEMPLE OF THE LOST SUN**
*EVERY RELIC REVEALS A SECRET.*
Visual: logo over the jungle temple background.
Small print: Playable demo · play money only.

### Slide 2 – Concept and theme
**An expedition inside every spin.**
- A cinematic adventure slot set at a forgotten sun temple (an invented world, no real culture).
- Cascading cluster wins move an expedition along a five-station path.
- The main bonus is *reached*, not rolled.
Visual: wide game screenshot with the track highlighted.

### Slide 3 – Main mechanics
**6×5 · Cluster Pays · Cascades**
- Clusters of 5 or more connected symbols win (horizontal and vertical).
- Winning symbols vanish, new ones fall – wins can chain.
- Wilds never drop: they are earned on the expedition.
- Maximum win 10,000× bet.
Visual: three-frame sequence win → dissolve → refill.

### Slide 4 – Expedition System
**Five stations. Five different powers.**
| Discovery | Treasure | Ancient Power | Relic | Temple |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Wilds | multiplier ×2 | sticky Explorer Wild | Sun Relic boosts the multiplier | Temple Bonus |
Every winning cascade is one step; Temple Keys add steps.
Visual: the track with all five medallions.

### Slide 5 – Free Spins
**Never start from zero.**
- 3 / 4 / 5 Compass: 8 / 10 / 12 Free Spins, retrigger +4.
- Every spin starts at the Base Camp; keys move the camp up.
- The sun bonus is kept for the whole feature.
- Temple seals lead to a Temple Finale with larger treasures.
Visual: temple-interior screenshot with multiplier and counter.

### Slide 6 – Temple Bonus
**Reach the Temple. Choose a chamber.**
- Three chambers: Sun, Jade, Moon.
- Prizes are set before the choice; the other chambers are revealed afterwards.
- A Relic Bonus opens a second chamber; the grand treasure pays up to 2,500× (Finale up to 10,000×).
Visual: chamber scene before and after the pick.

### Slide 7 – Visual identity and player experience
**Stone, gold, jade and turquoise – and one sun.**
- Two worlds: jungle approach and temple interior.
- Three speed modes, portrait and landscape, English and German.
- Rules, paytable and a replay of every round built in.
Visual: collage of logo, symbols, relic, both backgrounds.
Small print: Art and audio are AI-generated presentation assets; rights for commercial use are being clarified.

### Slide 8 – Technical status
**A finished game loop on a clean core.**
- Game engine separate from presentation; every round reproducible from a seed.
- Web demo on Phaser 3 / TypeScript; 86 automated tests; browser tests in ten formats.
- Not yet built: server integration, wallet, round recovery, market settings.
Small print: Automated tests in Chromium; owner-tested on iPhone 15 Pro; no certification.
