**TEMPLE OF THE LOST SUN** – provider trailer, target length 75 seconds (range 60–90)
Date: 25 November 2026 · status: script ready, **no footage recorded yet**

Principles: only real gameplay from the demo, recorded at Normal speed. Selected rounds come from
fixed seeds and are labelled as such in the end card. No invented wins, no altered probabilities,
no numbers on screen that are not in the fact sheet. No voice-over is required; on-screen text
carries the message. Audio: the game's own music and effects (subject to the rights question – see
the note at the end).

## Not to be said or shown

"Certified", "approved", "96 % RTP" without "target", win frequencies, market or player claims,
logos or names of providers, real-money balances.

## Rights note

The trailer would publish AI-generated images and audio. Their use for promoting a real-money
product is one of the unresolved legal questions (`PROJECT_AUDIT/IP/`). For private provider
meetings this is a presentation of a demo; **public release of a trailer should wait** until the
rights are cleared or the assets replaced.
